## Synthesis

The portfolio is strong in local execution, modular experimental architectures, controlled interventions, checkpoints and explicit result records. It already spans biological inspiration, memory carriers, synthetic evolution, program libraries, neural composition, exact verification, agent tools and source-to-experiment workbenches. Combining all of those as subsystems would make causal attribution harder and duplicate existing projects.

The narrower opportunity is to study **which connections are justified by the available evidence**. That question is grounded in actual semantic-map, information-availability and bypassed-test failures across the portfolio. CROSSWIRE uses those lessons to formulate a new experiment; it does not claim to repair the upstream projects or subsume their capabilities.

The reviews deliberately retain negative and incomplete evidence. Examples include Tessera's unsupported ratification benefit, Expanse's unchanged answer score, Ghost's failed proxy replication, Fly's unestablished 22-module advantage and Poseidon's rejected closed-loop adapter. These make meaningful controls and falsification criteria essential from the first build.

## Reuse policy

Implement the new instrument independently and cite ideas and concrete failure cases. Do not copy upstream code, trained weights, game content, biological graphs or generated datasets as an implicit part of this project. If a future stage imports an artifact, inspect that exact artifact's licence and provenance separately. The source review's licence inventory is not a legal determination or blanket permission.
