02 / INSIDE THE RELEASE

Plan. Preserve. Check.

01 / BUDGETED EXPERIMENTS

Look ahead before querying.

Depth-two experiment trees weigh hypothetical responses against a reset-and-action budget. The worker executes the checked root query, observes the response, then replans.

02 / EXPLICIT AMBIGUITY

Keep the candidate set visible.

Flat and factored backends represent the same finite adapter class. Structural factor counts describe representation; they do not measure memory compression or establish end-to-end scaling.

03 / INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE

Reconstruct the proposed tree.

SEMANTIC-CORRUPTION NEGATIVE CONTROL

Raw agreement has a blind spot.

Hidden meanings can change while raw observations stay compatible with the public model. The monitor may admit a plan even when the private semantic audit finds a hazard.

All four semantic-corruption control runs were admitted and hazardous.

WHAT THE GUARANTEES REQUIRE

  • The target belongs to the declared finite model class.
  • Behavior is stationary and resets are deterministic.
  • Semantic anchors are truthful.

Observable compatibility leaves model inclusion unknown. Candidate counts are not calibrated harm probabilities. Neither matching outputs nor a content hash establishes semantic grounding or real-world safety.
